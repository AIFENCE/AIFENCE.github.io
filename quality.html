Quality tier

Scores AI-generated artifacts against production quality controls before sensitive enforcement or transport runs.

The Quality tier is the first gate in the composed fence. A failed admission artifact is stopped before Guard evaluates the action.

#Two explicit modes

Admission is the fast deterministic synchronous gate used by /v1/fence/submit and /v1/quality/evaluate. Deep Quality 2.0 is the broader family-native runtime exposed explicitly through /v1/quality/deep/plan.

#Admission findings

Admission findings are machine-readable and stable enough for clients and regression tooling: identifier, severity, evidence, remediation, evaluator version and profile.

Example IDPurpose
AQ-COMPLETE-001Completeness failure
AQ-TEMPLATE-001Unresolved template/placeholder signal
AQ-GROUNDING-001Grounding evidence failure
