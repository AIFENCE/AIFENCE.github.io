Tier

Guard

The enforcement plane for policy, capabilities, approvals, detectors, evidence, and auditability.

Core questionIs this action permitted?

Responsibilities

  • Issues and consumes capability tokens for scoped authority.
  • Evaluates policies, risk, approvals, data classes, and destructive actions.
  • Supports agents, tools, providers, delegations, budgets, incidents, traces, and audit anchors.
  • Cannot silently fail open when enforcement is unavailable.

In the fence flow

This tier participates in POST /v1/fence/submit and contributes a stage-specific result to the single response receipt. The next tier only executes when the current stage permits it.
