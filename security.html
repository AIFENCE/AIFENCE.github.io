Security / externalized authority

Agents propose.
Guard decides.

AIFENCE moves sensitive authorization outside the agent process, binds policy to tenant and exact action, and keeps required control failures fail-closed.

Threat modelFailure semantics

Fail closed

Guard cannot fail open. Required database, audit, signing, and policy failures preserve denial rather than inventing authority.

Tenant boundaries

Identity and Bus workspaces remain tenant-scoped; knowing a receiver or message identifier does not grant cross-tenant access.

Exact action

Capabilities can bind tool, operation, resource, arguments, TTL, and usage rather than granting broad ambient permission.

Priority threats

Regression-backed boundaries.

AIFENCE maps security objectives to explicit controls and tests rather than relying on a generic “agent safety” claim.

Confused deputy

Mandatory Guard stage + exact-action capabilities.

Cross-tenant access

Tenant-scoped identity, storage, and Bus workspaces.

Replay with changed content

Idempotency binds sender, receiver, and payload digest.

Tampered wire

Frozen wire v2 schema and shared cross-language TCK.

Audit tampering

Per-tenant hash-chain/signing evidence and independently anchorable checkpoints.
