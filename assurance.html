Coverage policy

AIFENCE gates line and branch coverage independently. The verified snapshot measured 83.17% line and 63.81% branch coverage, with separate critical-module floors for the fence flow, Guard auth/enforcement/crypto/policy, Bus protocol/wire codec, and Quality admission.

Deep-assurance layer

Longer-running 5,000-case fuzzing, differential fuzzing, chaos, mutation testing, SBOM/provenance publication, and the PostgreSQL/Redis/Kafka/RabbitMQ/container matrix are additional release assurance. Results should be published here only after they run against the exact tagged tree.
