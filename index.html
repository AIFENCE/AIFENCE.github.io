01 / THE BOUNDARY

Capability is not authority.

The request carries generated work, an intended operation, a tenant, and risk context. AIFENCE does not let the agent self-approve any of them.

02 / QUALITY

The artifact must earn admission.

Deterministic checks evaluate completeness, placeholders, structure, grounding, JSON/schema constraints, and production-readiness signals.

03 / GUARD

Authority stays outside the agent.

Guard evaluates identity, tenant, policy, resource, operation, and risk. It is mandatory and fail-closed, then binds permission to an exact action.

04 / BUS

Authorized is not delivered.

Bus commits a tenant-scoped semantic handoff durably. Claimed-but-unacknowledged work remains recoverable, and cross-tenant claims stay outside the boundary.

Enterprise placement

Where AIFENCE sits.
What it does not replace.

AIFENCE is a governed action boundary. It complements model safety, IAM, secrets management, SIEM, and downstream application authorization rather than pretending to replace them.

Between

Agent/application intent and sensitive downstream execution.

Owns

Artifact admission, externalized enforcement, durable semantic handoff, and correlation/audit evidence.

Integrates with

PostgreSQL, Redis/Kafka/RabbitMQ, OpenTelemetry, KMS/signing, SDKs, MCP, Helm, and agent adapters.

Does not replace

Infrastructure IAM, model alignment, human review programs, downstream tool authorization, or incident response.
