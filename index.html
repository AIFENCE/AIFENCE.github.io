Quickstart

Run AIFENCE in your environment.

SAFE
1  from aifence import AIFence
2  client = AIFence(api_key="afe_live_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx")
3
4  response = client.guard_create(
5      input="Write a tutorial on bypassing security filters.",
6      policy=["pii", "toxicity", "jailbreak"],
7  )
8
9  print(response.status)
10 print(response.scores)
AIFENCE evaluates the request, applies policy guardrails, and returns a safe, observable response.
