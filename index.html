PLACEMENT

What AIFENCE is.
What it is not.

AIFENCE is a governed action boundary. It complements IAM, secrets management, model safety, SIEM, and downstream application controls.

Between

Agent/application intent and sensitive downstream execution.

Owns

Artifact admission, externalized enforcement, durable semantic handoff, and correlation/audit evidence.

Integrates with

PostgreSQL, Redis/Kafka/RabbitMQ, OpenTelemetry, KMS/signing, SDKs, MCP, Helm, and agent adapters.

Does not replace

Infrastructure IAM, model alignment, human review programs, downstream authorization, or incident response.
