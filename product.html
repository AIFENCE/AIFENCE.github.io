Product / governed execution

One boundary
around agent action.

AIFENCE separates four claims that agent systems often blur together: Is the work good enough? Is the action allowed? Was it durably handed off? Can you prove what happened?

One correlation identity and truthful completion receipt.

Product contract

Quality → Guard → Bus

Every tier owns a separate invariant. A downstream success cannot rewrite an upstream failure, and an authorization cannot be represented as delivery before persistence succeeds.

Quality

Stops incomplete, placeholder, structurally invalid, or insufficiently grounded artifacts before sensitive action evaluation.

Guard

Evaluates principal, tenant, policy, resource, operation, and risk; cannot be configured fail-open.

Bus

Commits the approved handoff durably, supports claim/ack and lease recovery, and enforces tenant-scoped workspaces.

Receipt

Reports handed_off only after the durable handoff and completion audit event actually exist.
