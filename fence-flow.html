Fence flow

The three tiers as one governed pipeline, with short-circuiting, resilience budgets, and one truthful receipt.

The fence flow is the executable product contract: one authenticated request is admitted by Quality, authorized by Guard, durably handed off by Bus, and completed with tamper-evident audit evidence.

text
POST /v1/fence/submit
   │
   ├── Quality admission ── fail ─▶ blocked_by_quality
   │        │ pass
   ├── Guard enforcement ── deny ─▶ blocked_by_guard
   │        │ allow
   ├── durable Bus handoff ─ fail ─▶ unavailable / configured advisory outcome
   │        │ committed
   └── fence.completed audit ──────▶ handed_off

#Outcomes

final_outcomeallowedMeaning
handed_offtrueAdmission passed, Guard allowed, durable handoff and completion audit succeeded
blocked_by_qualityfalseAdmission rejected the artifact
blocked_by_guardfalseGuard denied/held the action
authorized_not_deliveredtrueOnly possible where Bus is explicitly advisory; authorization did not become a false delivery claim

#Receipt

The receipt carries one correlation identity across the three stages and audit trail.

json
{
  "request_id": "req_…",
  "allowed": true,
  "final_outcome": "handed_off",
  "stages": {
    "quality": {"mode":"admission","profile":"default","passed":true},
    "guard": {"outcome":"allow","reason_codes":["GUARD_ALLOW"],"matched_rule":"baseline:read-only-low-risk"},
    "bus": {"delivered":true,"workspace":"tenant:…","message_id":"M…"}
  },
  "audit": {"event_id":"…","sequence":42,"event_hash":"…","signing_key_id":"…"}
}

#Resilience

Guard is always fail closed. Quality and Bus are fail closed by default and may only be made advisory deliberately. Database, signing requirements and completion-audit persistence fail closed. Optional telemetry/broker fan-out may degrade without changing an authorization decision.
