Quality modes

Admission is the bounded synchronous gate; Deep Quality 2.0 plans the broader family-native evaluation and evidence requirements.

#Admission

POST /v1/fence/submit and POST /v1/quality/evaluate use the deterministic admission evaluator. It answers whether an artifact is sufficiently complete and structurally sound to enter the governed action path.

Response fieldMeaning
modeadmission
profileThe admission profile used
evaluator_versionThe evaluator version
findings[]Stable ID, severity, evidence and remediation

#Deep Quality 2.0

The repository's quality/ source pack is the broader Quality 2.0 runtime. POST /v1/quality/deep/plan bridges to the built runtime and returns the family-native evaluation/evidence plan. It is explicit and is not silently run for every fence submission.

#Client contract

Clients should branch on explicit mode/profile metadata. An admission pass must not be represented as proof that the entire 1,000-control Quality corpus executed.
